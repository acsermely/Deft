Wallpaper Clock

To use this clock as your wallpaper, open the launcher settings, find Wallpaper Clock and tap Review next to "Be your wallpaper". Then set the live wallpaper on Android's preview screen.

The wallpaper runs only while your home or lock screen is visible. Tap the home screen to see a ripple. To stop it, remove the permission on the app's card; then choose your own picture again in Android Settings under Wallpaper.
